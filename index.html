The problem

In international trade, the invoice, packing list and bill of lading rarely tell the same story. Mismatched quantities, weights, values or container numbers are discovered too late — at the port, at customs, or during payment.

⚙️

The solution

DocuCheck AI checks all three documents automatically. It separates confirmed discrepancies from missing information, scores each finding from Critical to Low, and drafts ready-to-review correction or clarification letters.

🌎

The focus

Built on 16 years of hands-on procurement experience. Starting with food products and raw materials, with Chile and Latin America as the first market. Next on the roadmap: veterinary certificates and other regulatory documents.
